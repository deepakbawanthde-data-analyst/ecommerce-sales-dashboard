Business Requirements

The objective of this project is to analyze ecommerce sales performance and track key business metrics to support data-driven decision making.

KPIs Tracked

➤ YTD Sales: Monitor overall revenue performance throughout the year
➤ QTD Sales: Track quarterly sales trends and fluctuations
➤ YTD Products Sold: Analyze total product movement
➤ YTD Reviews: Evaluate customer feedback and satisfaction

Dashboard Visuals

➤ YTD Sales by Month (Line Chart): Analyze monthly sales trends and seasonality
➤ YTD Sales by Week (Column Chart): Identify short-term sales fluctuations
➤ Sales by Product Category (Heat Map): Compare performance across categories
➤ Top 5 Products by YTD Sales (Bar Chart): Identify highest revenue-generating products
➤ Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews (Bar Chart): Understand most popular and highly reviewed products
